# Two Chairs

- **URL:** https://www.twochairs.com (200, canonical)
- **Title:** "Two Chairs — Mental health care, made for you"
- **Meta description:** none present
- **Platform:** Webflow
- **Peer relevance:** HIGH on craft, MEDIUM on service model (therapy-led rather than prescribing). **This is the best-designed site in the peer set** and the closest reference for the warmth Newpoint needs.

## Layout system
Webflow container, generous and calm. Fewer sections than any other peer (six major blocks against Meru's twelve). The restraint is the design decision: Two Chairs says less and gives each statement more room. Section rhythm is slow and even.

## Hero pattern
Headline "Mental health care, made for you". Four words, one idea, no insurance qualifier, no speed claim. The most confident hero in the set. Primary CTA "Get started", secondary "Book appointment" in nav.

## Section flow (top to bottom)
1. Hero: "Mental health care, made for you"
2. "Scientifically proven to help" (evidence claim, placed before any product explanation)
3. "One team for comprehensive care"
4. "What people are saying" (testimonials)
5. "Find care that fits your life"
6. "Getting started"
7. Footer: Our story / Media & press / Insights / Reviews / Careers / For health plans

**Most reusable structure:** the six-section homepage. Every other peer runs eight to twelve sections. Two Chairs proves a behavioral-health homepage does not need condition grids, stat bands, and feature quads to convert. For a two-provider practice this is the more appropriate scale by a wide margin.

## Typography
- Display: **Feijoa** (a warm, humanist serif with soft terminals)
- Body and UI: system sans stack (Helvetica Neue / Arial fallbacks in the Webflow base)

Feijoa is the single best type decision in the peer set. It is warm without being decorative, and it carries authority without the coldness of a transitional serif.

## Color
| Hex | Role |

| `#A28978` | Warm tan / clay, secondary |
| `#ECE6D9` | Cream, section grounds |
| `#F6F4EC` | Bone, page ground |
| `#D37E31` | Burnt orange accent |

| `#686868` | Body grey |

The full **sage / clay / cream / burnt-orange** system. This is the category-defining palette that Rula, Cerebral, Meru, and Talkiatry all orbit in their own ways. Note `#191717` rather than pure black: the near-black is warmed, which is what keeps the whole palette from going cold.

## Components
Clinician cards with real names and license status ("Matthew Stephan, Fully licensed", "Janet Dion, Fully licensed", "Moet Monroe, Fully licensed"). Testimonial module. Pricing and insurance as a named nav item rather than buried. Referral and health-plan partner paths in the footer.

**The named-and-licensed clinician card is the most directly transferable component to Newpoint.** Two Chairs shows the person, the name, and the license status together, which is exactly the trust triad a two-provider practice needs.

## Motion and interaction
Webflow easing set: `cubic-bezier(.25,.46,.45,.94)` (ease-out quad), `cubic-bezier(.455,.03,.515,.955)` (ease-in-out quad), `cubic-bezier(.645,.045,.355,1)` (ease-in-out cubic). Character: gentle, symmetric, unhurried. No spring, no overshoot, no scroll-driven sequence. The motion matches the brand's calm, which is the correct call for this category.

Radius scale is tight and restrained: `0.125rem`, `0.25rem`, `0.5rem`, `0.75rem`, and `0`. Nothing is pill-shaped or heavily rounded. Sharp-leaning geometry against warm color is what makes it read as considered rather than soft.

## Imagery
Warm, naturally lit, editorial. People photographed as people, not as stock models performing wellness. Real clinician headshots.

## Conversion mechanics
"Get started" is the single CTA intent, held consistently. Evidence ("Scientifically proven to help") is placed above the product explanation, which inverts the peer norm and works. Testimonials do heavy lifting in position four. Insurance is present but not shouted, appearing as a nav item rather than a hero qualifier.

## Tone of voice
Calm, adult, unhurried. Short sentences. No urgency language, no scarcity, no cost anxiety. The voice assumes the reader is capable and treats them accordingly. This is the register Newpoint should target.

## Steal
- Six-section homepage discipline
- Warm near-black (`#191717`) instead of pure black or cool slate
- Named clinician cards with license status
- Evidence claim before product explanation
- Tight radius scale against warm color
- Calm symmetric easing that matches the brand promise

## Avoid
- Missing meta description
- Insurance is arguably under-surfaced for a practice whose patients will ask about it first
