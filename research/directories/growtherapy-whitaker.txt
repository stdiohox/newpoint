Do you feel overwhelmed and pulled in different directions, unable to be happy? Are you struggling to keep up with your daily activities, relationship, work, and health? Is it hard to be good to yourself and breathe in and out?

I help high stressed manage anxiety and avoid burnout. Together, we can identify what works and what is not working to get you in balance. We'll use an evidence-based therapeutic approach, including psychotropic medications, to address your needs, helping you develop a stronger relationship with yourself. 

Using technology-based therapeutic tools such as telehealth in behavioral health services to see patients via telehealth on an expanded schedule (weekends evenings and holidays by request).


I'm here for you, whether you're dealing with cycles of anxiety, depression, mood disorder, PTSD, panic attacks, irritability, anger issues, psychosis or other mental health issues, I am here for you. Let's work together.

Treatment methods
Compassion Focused

I have experience with compassion-focused treatment for people with mental health problems that involves guided visualizations, compassionate imagery, and other techniques to break the routine of self-criticism and improve emotional regulation. This method is used in my practice to help my patients learn the key skills required to develop the key aspects and attributes of compassion, i.e., care for well-being, sensitivity, distress tolerance, empathy, and non-judgment attitudes towards oneself.
