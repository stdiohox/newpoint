# Services — Structured List & Gap Analysis

## Important scope note
The brief for this audit asked to flag gaps "vs. a standard home-healthcare menu." Based on the actual site content, **this practice is not a home-health agency** (i.e., not offering Medicare-certified skilled nursing visits, home health aides, PT/OT/speech therapy in the home, wound care, etc.). It is an **outpatient/telehealth psychiatric mental-health nurse-practitioner practice** — psychiatric evaluation, medication management, and related behavioral-health services, delivered in-office and via telehealth. The comparison below is against a standard **outpatient psychiatric / behavioral-health practice** menu, which is the correct peer set for what's actually on the site. If the client does *also* offer traditional home-health services that just aren't reflected on the site, that's a question to put back to them — the current site would need to be told that directly, since nothing on it suggests home-health-agency services exist today.

Includes: initial comprehensive psychiatric evaluation, risk-factor identification, diagnosis via assessment, treatment planning combining psychotherapy modalities + psychopharmacology, collaboration with other professionals, ongoing progress evaluation/support/education.

## Gaps vs. a standard outpatient psychiatric/behavioral-health menu

- **No named therapy modalities.** "Individual counseling" and "psychotherapy modalities" are mentioned generically, but no specific modality (CBT, DBT, EMDR, supportive therapy, etc.) is named — common on competitor sites and useful for both SEO and patient trust.
- **No stated age range / population served.** Nothing on the site says whether they see adults only, or also children/adolescents/geriatric patients ("across the lifespan" is mentioned once in one provider's bio, but it's not stated as an official practice policy).
- **No substance use / addiction treatment service**, despite one provider's bio explicitly stating 11 years of addiction-focused nursing experience — this looks like an unlisted capability that could be a service line if the client actually offers it.
- **No crisis / emergency protocol.** No guidance on what a patient in crisis should do (e.g., "call 911 or 988 if this is an emergency" — increasingly a standard, and often required-feeling, disclaimer for behavioral-health sites).
- **No online scheduling/booking tool** — intake is phone/email/contact-form only.
- **No new-patient information** (what to bring, forms to complete in advance, what the first telehealth session looks like practically).
- **No description of the group offerings** ("skill building groups," "support groups" are named as options but never described — cadence, format, topics).
- **No pricing/self-pay rate** given even though "session fee" and "sliding scale" are confirmed to exist.
- **Testimonials section literally says "Coming soon"** — no social proof of outcomes/patient experience exists yet.
- **No FAQ section anywhere on the site.**
